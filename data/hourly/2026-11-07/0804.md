### Steps to reproduce
1. Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → turn on Smart Invert.
2. Settings → Accessibility → Zoom → turn on Zoom, set Zoom Filter to "Low Light", Zoom Region to Full Screen, and zoom level 1.0x (or the lowest possible).
3. Open a white page, e.g. Safari or Books.
4. Compare the screen with Low Light on and off.

### Expected (iOS 26 and earlier)
- White background → near black
- Black text → dim grey
- Result: darker than Smart Invert alone

### Actual (iOS 27)
- White background → light grey (~70%)
- Black text → full white
- Result: brighter and lower contrast than Smart Invert alone

### Likely root cause
The order of the two filters seems to have been swapped in the display/compositing pipeline.

- Old order: Smart Invert → Low Light (invert, then dim)
