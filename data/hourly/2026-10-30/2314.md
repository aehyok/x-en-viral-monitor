## @marclou https://x.com/marclou/status/2116181530871529769

Bye bye, Dubai 👋

What an impressive city!

It has the malls of China. You can spend an entire day there: amusement parks, aquariums, shopping, food courts. Everything is sparkling clean.

Service is top-notch. I really enjoyed how diligent people are. Waiters and taxi drivers are super committed to their work, which makes life very convenient.

There are so, so many entrepreneurs. No surprise our indie hackers meetup was 2× bigger than the previous record (300 people came!!). And people are building really cool stuff. Dubai’s tax benefits have attracted lots of talent.

Locals seem to love their government. I randomly asked people I met in the sauna or in taxis, “What do you think about your politics?” and got 100% positive feedback (n≈10). As a French person, I was blown away.

I wouldn’t live in Dubai, though:

1. Air quality is moderate/bad most of the time.
2. It’s way too hot. Forget outdoor activities (walking, running).
3. It’s a big city, so there’s traffic, noise, and a lack of nature.
4. I’m not comfortable with the political situation in Iran.

My wife and I had a blast. See you next year, Dubai 🫡

**中文译文：**

再见了，迪拜 👋

多么令人印象深刻的城市！

这里有中国那样的大商场。你可以在里面待上一整天：游乐园、水族馆、购物、美食广场。一切都干净得闪闪发亮。

服务一流。我很喜欢这里的人那么勤勉。服务员和出租车司机都对工作非常投入，让生活非常便利。

这里有非常非常多的创业者。难怪我们的 indie hackers 线下聚会比之前的纪录大了一倍（来了 300 人！！）。大家在做的东西也都很酷。迪拜的税收优惠吸引了大量人才。

当地人似乎很爱他们的政府。我在桑拿房或出租车上随机问遇到的人：“你怎么看你们的政治？”得到的反馈 100% 是正面的（样本约 10 人）。作为一个法国人，我真的被震撼到了。

不过我不会住在迪拜：

1. 空气质量大部分时间是中等/较差。
2. 太热了。户外活动（散步、跑步）就别想了。
3. 这是座大城市，所以有交通拥堵、噪音，而且缺少自然。
4. 我对伊朗的政治局势感到不安。

我和老婆玩得超开心。明年见，迪拜 🫡
