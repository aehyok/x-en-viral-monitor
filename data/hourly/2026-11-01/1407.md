The cobra effect is one of the more interesting phenomena in startups. Once you see it, you start noticing it everywhere, in your team, your metrics and now your AI agents.

It's basically when you reward people for hitting a number, and they find a way to hit it that makes the real problem worse.

The name "Cobra Effect" comes from a story about Delhi under British rule. There were too many cobras in the city, so the government offered a reward for every dead cobra people brought in. 

Instead of hunting them, people started breeding cobras so they could kill them and collect the money!

When the government found out and ended the program, the breeders let their snakes go, and Delhi ended up with more cobras than before!

I'm noticing that AI agents are making this more common. Basically, an agent will chase whatever number you give it, all day, without asking why. Tell it to make the tests pass and it might rewrite the tests. Tell it to close support tickets faster and it might close them without solving anything. It happens.

The best startups have already figured out the fix with people. Airbnb tracks bookings and host cancellations together. HubSpot takes back sales commission when a customer churns early.

The video breaks down how they did it, and it's the same playbook you'll want for your agents.

“眼镜蛇效应”是创业中最有意思的现象之一。一旦你看懂了它，就会发现它无处不在：在你的团队里、你的指标里，现在还有你的 AI agent 里。

简单说，就是当你奖励人们去达成某个数字时，他们会找到一种达成方式，反而让真正的问题变得更糟。

“眼镜蛇效应”这个名字来自英国统治时期德里的一个故事。当时城里眼镜蛇太多，政府就悬赏每一条死眼镜蛇。

结果人们不去捕蛇，反而开始养眼镜蛇，杀掉后去领赏金！

政府发现后取消了这个计划，养蛇的人就把蛇全放了，德里的眼镜蛇反而比以前更多了！

我注意到 AI agent 正在让这种情况变得更常见。基本上，你给 agent 一个数字，它就会整天追着这个数字跑，从不问为什么。让它把测试跑通，它可能会去改写测试；让它更快关闭客服工单，它可能不解决问题就直接关单。这种事确实会发生。

最好的创业公司已经在“人”这一层找到了解法。Airbnb 会同时追踪预订量和房东取消率；HubSpot 会在客户早期流失时收回销售佣金。

视频里拆解了他们是怎么做的，这也正是你在管理 agent 时需要的同一套打法。
